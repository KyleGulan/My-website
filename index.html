04 / About

Stories first.
Pixels second.

I’m Gilmar, a YouTube thumbnail designer. Before I open any design tool, I ask one question: what’s the story, and what single image would make someone stop scrolling for it?

From there I build around one clear focal point, big readable text and strong contrast. AI helps me explore directions fast, and my own eye decides which one is good enough to ship.

05 / FAQ

Quick answers.

What do you need from me?

Your video title, the niche, and any reference or channel style you want me to follow.

What file will I get?

A 1280 × 720 image (PNG or JPG), ready to upload to YouTube.

Can I request changes?

Yes. After the first concept, send me your notes and I’ll refine it.

How fast can you deliver?

Tell me your deadline when you message me and I’ll confirm what’s possible.

Do you use AI?

Yes, as a production tool. Composition, hierarchy, contrast and the final design decisions are done by me.
